Security research

// defensive + authorized offensive
DEFENSIVE FIRST

Harden what you run

I prioritize defensive posture: reduce exposure, enforce sane defaults, and build systems that fail safely. Security becomes a property of the system—not a last-minute patch.

  • Threat-aware thinking: identify likely paths, reduce attack surface
  • Operational resilience: detection + response readiness
  • Secure-by-default habits: least privilege, explicit controls
AUTHORIZED OFFENSIVE

Validate with permission

When appropriate and authorized, I validate assumptions by testing for real-world failure modes—so remediation is grounded in reality.

  • Authorized offensive testing to confirm defensive coverage
  • Actionable findings: prioritized, reproducible, fix-oriented
  • Documentation that helps teams learn (not just “pass”)

My default mode is: observe → instrument → isolate → fix. If something “just happens,” I assume it’s measurable and therefore removable.

Background

/history

I’m a former Microsoft engineer who worked a little bit on Windows and a former Google Web Administrator who made sure Google.com was always running. Those roles gave me a close look at what “software at scale” really means: messy environments, long tails of edge cases, and systems that can become policy by default.

Today I focus on security research and building tools that strip away complexity—especially where telemetry, background services, and forced updates create risk and unpredictability.
